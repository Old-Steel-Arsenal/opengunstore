### Version 1.1.0 — November 17, 2026

#### New

- **Pre-orders.** Set how many of an item can be pre-ordered, and an expected date, on the item. At the counter a customer can pay in full or leave a deposit; the money is held until the goods are handed over, and a cancelled pre-order is refunded in full. When the goods arrive they are put by for the oldest order waiting, and a gun gets its serial number then. On the website a pre-order item can be bought and paid in full, and the order waits until it ships.
- **Place order on web orders.** On the Pending Order page, a web order under To place with distributor now has a Place order button. The order is not sent if the web order has since been refunded, cancelled or put on hold.

#### Improved

- **Customer status is Active or Banned.** Inactive and Prospect are gone; customers who had them are now Active, with the old status noted on their timeline. A few settings a single store never uses are hidden on the customer form unless already set.
- **Extensions update without a restart.** Installing or updating an extension no longer takes the POS down for a minute.
- **Extension pages in the sidebar.** An extension can now put its pages in an existing sidebar section, such as Compliance, instead of a section of its own.

#### Fixed

- **A gun held by a web or GunBroker order cannot be sold at the counter.** Before, only scanning the gun was refused; picking its serial from the list or resuming a saved ticket went through. Now every way of selling it is refused, and the message names the order holding it.
