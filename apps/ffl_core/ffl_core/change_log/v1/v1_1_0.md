### Version 1.1.0 — November 18, 2026

#### New

- **Products with options on the website.** An item with variants (for example one lower in several fits and trigger options) now goes to the website as one product. The customer picks the option on the product page. Each option keeps its own price, stock and pre-order slots, and a web order lands on the exact item the customer picked. Pushing the main item sends all of its options; changing one option's price or stock updates only that option.

#### Fixed

- **Signed 4473 copies are saved again.** Since the last platform upgrade the POS stopped saving its own copy of each signed FastBound 4473. New ones are saved again, and the ones missed since then are fetched within the hour after this update. Nothing was lost: every form stayed in FastBound.
