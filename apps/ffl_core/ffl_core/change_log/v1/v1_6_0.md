### Version 1.6.0 — November 25, 2026

#### New

- **Price Hub.** A new page under Purchasing for comparing what each of your distributors charges. Look a product up by UPC, SKU or name (or scan it) to see every distributor's cost and stock, what a unit leaves when sold at MAP, how each cost has moved over time, and what the store has on hand, on order, sold lately and paid on its last receipts. Add puts the quantity you choose into the Distributor Orders cart. With nothing looked up, the page lists costs that dropped and stock that is running out.
- **CFD number for California dealers.** A California FFL's state CFD license number can now be typed in on the customer, the supplier or the FFL record. Enter it once and every record for that dealer shows it.

#### Improved

- **The cart on Distributor Orders stays put.** What you add is kept in your browser, so leaving the page or reloading it no longer empties the cart, and Price Hub adds to the same one.
- **GunBroker orders name the receiving dealer sooner.** When an order comes in for a dealer the POS has not seen before, it looks the license up right away and the pending-order card shows the dealer's name instead of waiting until the gun is disposed.
- **The built-in AI assistant knows how a gun store counts.** Asked about guns in stock, it now leaves out transfer, consigned and held guns, and it does not repeat Social Security numbers, full ID numbers or dates of birth.
