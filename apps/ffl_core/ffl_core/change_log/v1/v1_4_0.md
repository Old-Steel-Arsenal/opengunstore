### Version 1.4.0 — November 23, 2026

#### New

- **Connect your WooCommerce store in one click.** In **WooCommerce Settings**, enter your store's address and click **Connect**, then approve on your WooCommerce site. The POS sets up the store key, the order notifications and, when the OpenGunstore plugin is installed on the store, the plugin's connection back to the POS. The Status section shows each step, and if one fails it names that step. A store that is already connected keeps working as it is.

#### Improved

- **The Sales Report costs a sold gun as of its sale.** When a gun is sold and later comes back in (bought back, traded in, or a customer's transfer gun the store then buys), re-running an old period no longer changes the cost of the earlier sale. A customer's transfer gun handed over at $0 stays at $0 cost on its ticket.
- **Latest Frappe and ERPNext.** The POS runs on the newest releases of the system it is built on, with their security and bug fixes.
- **Customer and supplier pages open on an Overview tab.** A saved customer or supplier now opens on a summary of their recent activity. Everything you used to see first is under **Details**.

#### Fixed

- **A gun invoiced again at $0 to its buyer is not counted twice.** When a sold gun comes back in transit and goes out again to the same customer on a $0 invoice, the Sales Report no longer shows it as a second sale at a loss.
- **A refund of a sale that gave change starts at what the customer paid.** Returning a register sale where change was given now fills in the sale amount as the refund, not the cash that was handed over.
