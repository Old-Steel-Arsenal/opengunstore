### Version 1.3.2 — November 20, 2026

#### New

- **Bring your inventory in from a CSV file.** A store that is starting on OpenGunstore can load its accessories, ammunition, optics and parts in one file: **Settings → Catalog Reference → Inventory Import**. Download the template, fill in one row per product (UPC, name, brand, part number, category, quantity, unit cost, price), pick the warehouse and the cut-over date, then click **Preview**. The preview shows what each row will do before anything is saved: match an item you have, create one from a distributor catalog, create a new one, or refuse the row with the reason. The import runs in the background and **Download Report** gives the result row by row. Firearms never go in the file; they come from FastBound or Receive Goods. The admin guide has a new page, **Move your inventory in**.

#### Improved

- **FastBound Import Bound Book books the guns as opening stock.** Each gun is now booked on the cut-over date you pick, at its FastBound cost, against the Temporary Opening account. Before, it was booked against Stock Adjustment, so the opening inventory showed up as a negative expense on the first day. Your bookkeeper clears Temporary Opening with a journal entry once the opening balances are in. Guns imported before this version are not changed.
- **No made-up cost for a gun.** A gun with no cost in FastBound is listed in the result and not imported. Before, it was booked at $0.01.
- **A large bound book no longer times out.** The import runs in the background, and the result opens when it is done. It lists errors, guns with no cost, acquisition types with no match, and guns to sort out by hand.

#### Fixed

- **A failed web payment no longer splits the customer's history.** When a web order's payment failed and the customer had a lead but no open deal, CRM made a new deal and left the customer's emails behind on the lead. Now the lead is converted into the deal, so the emails and notes are in one place.
