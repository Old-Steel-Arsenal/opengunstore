### Version 1.7.0 — November 25, 2026

#### New

- **Matches on a lead or deal.** The side panel of a CRM lead or deal now lists what the store has in stock for that customer: the items on the record, what fits their open wants, and what fits what they said they are looking for. Each line shows the register's price and how many can be sold now.
- **Daily reply list.** A manager can turn on a morning email that lists the leads and deals where the customer wrote last and nobody has answered, longest wait first.
- **Drafted replies.** With the store's AI extension set up, Draft reply on a lead or deal writes an answer to the customer's message with current prices and stock for you to read, change and send. A manager can also have the draft written as the mail arrives.
- **Wants from what the customer said.** A manager can turn on wants that are made by themselves when a customer asks for something the store does not have, so the arrival of a matching gun raises a task.

#### Improved

- **Hold a gun without leaving the deal.** The Hold dialog now lets you pick the gun by its serial number, lists the in-stock guns that fit what the customer asked for, and lets you choose how long the hold lasts: 24 hours, 48 hours, 3 days or 7 days.
- **Website questions show as mail.** A question sent from the website now appears on its lead or deal as a received email, so it can be answered with Reply like any other mail.
- **CRM tasks open in CRM.** A lead or deal on My tasks now opens its CRM page.

#### Fixed

- **A gun that could not be held is no longer left on the deal.** The dialog now says why the hold was refused.
- **Customer memory keeps up again.** Records waiting for their memory to be written are no longer skipped.
