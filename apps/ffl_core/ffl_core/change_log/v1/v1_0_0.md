### Version 1.0.0 — November 16, 2026

The version number moves from 0.10 to 1.0. The POS works the same way.

#### New

- **Draft reply and Insert price in CRM.** On a lead or deal, Insert price puts a product and today's price into your reply; with an AI extension turned on, Draft reply writes a first draft for you to edit and send.
- **Marketing User role.** Someone with this role can work on the marketing list from an assistant connected to the POS: find contacts, add or remove tags, record consent, honour an unsubscribe and close reminder requests. They see nothing else in the POS.

#### Improved

- **Finished leads and deals leave My tasks.** When a deal is won or lost, or a lead is converted to a deal or moved to Other mail, its assignments come off My tasks. They come back if the deal is reopened.
- **Web orders waiting on a distributor.** A paid web order that still needs a distributor order now shows on the Pending Order page under To place with distributor, so it is not left unsent.

#### Fixed

- **Reply on GunBroker member mail.** Reply on a mail from a GunBroker member now opens with the address filled in instead of failing.
