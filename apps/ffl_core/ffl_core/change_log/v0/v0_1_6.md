### Version 0.1.6 — October 17, 2026

#### New

- **Customer alerts at the counter.** When you pick a customer at the register, in Create Order, in a trade-in or in Receive Goods, you now see their status, alerts and any ID that has expired or expires soon.
- **Banned customers are stopped at the register.** A banned customer cannot be rung up; a manager can override it for 30 minutes and must give a reason. Web and GunBroker orders from a banned buyer still come in, marked with a red badge.
- **Duplicate customer check.** Before you add a new customer, you see any existing customer with the same email, phone or licence number and can use that one instead.
- **Agreements and waivers.** Customers sign range waivers and other agreements on the store's signing tablet, by QR code on their own phone, or by email. They get the signed copy by email, and you can record a paper copy instead.
- **Agreements the sale needs.** An agreement can be optional, needed for certain items, or needed for every sale, and some are signed once for each firearm sold. The register asks for a missing one before checkout, and the new Agreements button shows them all.
- **Signing tablets.** Pair a tablet once and send agreements to it from the counter.
- **Switch user.** Click the button at the bottom left of the sidebar, pick yourself and type your own password to take over the screen.
- **Screen lock.** After 5 minutes with no one using it, the counter screen locks and shows the staff list. A manager can change the time or turn the lock off in FFL Settings.
- **Gunsmith work orders.** Take in a customer's gun for repair, have them sign the work authorization, take a deposit, tell them when it is ready, and ring up the pick-up with the deposit taken off. You choose whether the gun goes in the bound book, and you can add it later.
