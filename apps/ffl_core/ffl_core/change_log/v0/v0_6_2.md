### Version 0.6.2 — October 31, 2026

#### New

- **Changing the buyer on a paid order.** When you change the buyer on an order that is already paid, Edit Order now asks what to do with the money: refund the old buyer, or carry the payment over to the new buyer. Carrying it over refunds nothing; if the new total is lower, only the difference is refunded, back to the card or cash the payer used. When the payment cannot be carried over, the page says why and asks you to send the order to the bookkeeper.

#### Fixed

- **Dealer sales no longer ask for a customer agreement.** Selling a used or Curio & Relic gun to a licensed dealer, including a consignment settlement, no longer stops for the used-gun agreement, which is written for retail buyers.
