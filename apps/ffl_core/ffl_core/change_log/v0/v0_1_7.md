### Version 0.1.7 — October 17, 2026

#### New

- **Gunsmith work orders at the register.** The Work Order button next to Trade-in takes a gun in, and anything the customer pays now goes into the sale and is checked out like any other, card included. Recent Orders › Work Orders lists the open jobs: take another payment, get the authorization signed, or Complete the job to confirm the charges and ring up the pick-up with what was paid taken off.
- **Counter PIN.** In the switch-user list, Set my PIN gives you a 4 to 6 digit PIN to switch in or unlock the screen; your password still works too. A manager can clear a PIN.

#### Improved

- **Switch user is in the user menu.** Click your name at the bottom left and pick Switch user.
- **Deposit refunds go back the way they were paid.** A leftover gunsmith deposit is refunded at the register, so a card payment goes back to the card.
- **Theme.** Switch between light and dark under your name › Settings › Appearance; the extra moon/sun button is gone.

#### Fixed

- **The screen no longer locks while you are using it.** Another page left open in the background used to lock the whole counter after a few minutes.
- **A locked screen can always be unlocked by the person signed in.**
- **No "No accounting entries" message** when a customer's gun is received into custody.
- **GunBroker orders keep syncing.** Orders with a long eFFL link stopped updating, so the dealer's FFL never showed; they update again, and the Pending Orders card shows "No FFL yet" and a short warning instead of an error message.
