### Version 0.2.1 — October 24, 2026

#### New

- **Peace-officer excise exemption (California stores).** When an active or retired peace officer, or the law enforcement agency they work for, buys a firearm or ammunition, tick the exemption on Create Order or tap LE Exempt at the register, pick who the buyer is and upload their CDTFA-230-FET certificate: the 11% excise tax comes off and sales tax is still charged.

#### Fixed

- **Paid web orders picked up in store keep their online payment.** Opening the pickup ticket at the register no longer swaps the online payment for the register's own, so the customer is not asked to pay again.
- **The cash drawer counts cash taken on an order that is not finished yet.** Cash taken as a part payment, or before the background check clears, now shows in the expected cash right away instead of only when the order is completed.
