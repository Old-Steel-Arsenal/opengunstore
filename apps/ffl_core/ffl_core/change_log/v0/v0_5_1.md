### Version 0.5.1 — October 29, 2026

#### Fixed

- **GunBroker orders stuck after Dispose.** An order whose invoice did not post after Dispose now shows a Create Invoice button on the Pending Order page instead of no buttons at all; once the invoice posts, Ship appears. Dispose also warns you when this happens.
- **GunBroker invoices no longer blocked by their own sale.** The check that stops a counter sale of a gun already sold on GunBroker no longer stops the GunBroker order's own invoice.
