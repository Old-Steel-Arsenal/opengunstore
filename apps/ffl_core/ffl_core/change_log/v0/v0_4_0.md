### Version 0.4.0 — October 28, 2026

#### New

- **Memberships page.** Customers → Memberships shows your members, plans and totals in one place: search members, sell a membership, edit a plan, and work through the members who need attention.
- **Manager approval for lower prices.** When a store turns it on, lowering a price at the counter asks a manager to approve with their PIN and a reason, and the sale records who approved it. It is off until a manager switches it on in FFL Settings.
- **Ship GunBroker orders before Dispose.** A paid GunBroker order can be shipped from Pending Orders right away, the same as a counter order.

#### Improved

- **New membership plans make their own products.** Saving a plan creates its product, and its registration fee product when there is a fee.
- **Freight estimates for Lipsey's.** Drop-ship orders from Lipsey's show an estimated freight charge, like RSR and Sports South.
- **RSR case prices.** Ordering a full case from RSR for the store is priced at RSR's case price.

#### Fixed

- **Sales staff can run the register.** A login with only the sales role can open a shift, ring up sales, take returns and close the register.
- **GunBroker dealer licences.** A dealer licence that GunBroker sends partly hidden is now filled in, so the order can be shipped and disposed.
