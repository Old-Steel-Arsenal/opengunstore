### Version 0.4.1 — October 28, 2026

#### Improved

- **One agreement for every gun in a sale.** When a customer buys several used guns at once, they sign one agreement that lists all of them, instead of one copy per gun.

#### Fixed

- **GunBroker listings after Dispose.** Disposing a GunBroker order now retires the gun's GunBroker listing, so it is no longer checked again afterwards.
