### Version 0.6.5 — November 5, 2026

#### New

- **Key in a card number without the terminal.** For a card that will not read, or an order taken over the phone, a manager can tick **Key in card number (no terminal)** when charging a card at the register, on Create Order, or in Pending Order → Record Payment. The card processor's own payment page opens; type the card there (the customer's name and address start filled in). The POS records the payment as soon as the card is approved, and it can be voided or refunded like any other card payment. Card-not-present rates apply, so use the terminal whenever the card is in hand.
- **Team mail in the CRM.** Mail customers send to the store's shared addresses now lands in the CRM once, where everyone on the sales team can see it, and the first person to answer a new one becomes its owner. Replies you send from the CRM ask the customer to answer the store's main address, so anyone can pick the thread up. Personal mail in your own mailbox stays out of the POS. Your administrator turns this on in Lead Mail Settings.
- **GunBroker orders: Record Payment.** Pending Order now has Record Payment with Credit Card, Zelle, ACH or Check/Money Order (no cash). A manager can key the buyer's card in on the payment page.

#### Fixed

- **Cash log on paid open orders.** Cash taken on an open order now shows in the cash log on the day it was taken, instead of the day the order was finished, so the log's running balance matches the drawer. The drawer's expected amount was always right.
