### Version 0.9.0 — November 13, 2026

#### New

- **Member prices.** A membership plan can now list what members pay: a percentage or an amount off, a fixed price, or another price list's price, for an item, an item group or a price list, on chosen days and with a daily limit. The register and Sales Invoices apply the member price by themselves and show the reason on the line; the customer's membership shows as a badge next to their name.
- **Assigned to you.** When someone assigns you a task, a box appears on whatever page you are on and lists what was assigned and by whom. Press *Got it* to say you saw it; the person who assigned it sees that on the document.
- **More than one card reader.** A store can now set up several card readers. Each register computer picks its reader once and remembers it, and the payment screen shows which reader a charge is on.
- **Sales Summary report.** Sales, discounts, returns, tax, shipping and payment methods for a date range on one page, also by category, register or employee, in the same numbers the Sales Report uses.
- **Guns with no serial number or a shared serial number.** The bound book import now brings in guns recorded with no serial number, and two guns that carry the same serial number, each as its own gun. These guns cannot be sold or listed online yet, and a gun with no serial number cannot be received at the counter yet.
- **Website connector.** A manager can let chosen staff read the store's website (orders, products) from an AI assistant, signed in with their POS account. It is off until the store sets it up.

#### Improved

- **Excise tax on reports.** The Sales Report no longer counts excise tax as sales tax: excise has its own column, shown only for periods that collected it.
- **AI connectors say which store they are.** With several stores connected, the assistant is told which store each connector works on, and a request to confirm a change names the store.
- **Card charge checks.** The daily check of card charges and the membership renewals now run as longer background jobs, and renewals continue in batches, so a busy day no longer cuts them short.

#### Fixed

- **Discounts on tickets opened for a customer.** A ticket opened from the range board or by an assistant no longer loses the discount percentage, and a lowered price there now asks for a manager's approval like one typed at the register.
