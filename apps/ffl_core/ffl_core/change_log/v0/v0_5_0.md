### Version 0.5.0 — October 29, 2026

#### New

- **Give card money back from the invoice.** Managers can void a card charge on an order the same day, or refund part or all of it to the card, from the Card refund button on the invoice; a return or price cut takes the amount off the sale, tax included.

#### Fixed

- **GunBroker sales caught between checks.** A gun sold on GunBroker is now reserved for its order even when the listing check saw the sale first.
- **Sports South store orders.** Orders shipped to the store now show and record Sports South's own store shipping charge instead of the drop-ship rate.
