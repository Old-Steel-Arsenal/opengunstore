### Version 0.7.2 — November 7, 2026

#### New

- **In-store customers on the shop's mailing list.** Turn on *Send customer emails to the shop* in WooCommerce Settings and, every hour, customers added or changed at the counter are sent to the website's marketing list. People who unsubscribed stay unsubscribed. Off until you turn it on.
- **Antique trade-ins.** The Trade-in dialog has an Antique box (receiver made before 1899). An antique is not booked into the bound book or sent to FastBound.
- **Follow up failed web payments.** When a website order's payment fails, the customer's CRM deal gets a note with the order and a pay link, and the team gets a task to contact them. Nothing is emailed to the customer. Needs the CRM deal-to-POS link turned on.

#### Improved

- **Email and Phone on the customer form.** The Details tab now shows the customer's email and phone and saves them to their main contact.
- **CRM deals find the buyer by full name.** When no email or phone matches, a customer with the same full name is offered as a guess for you to confirm.
- **GunBroker tracking.** Tracking for a label bought outside the POS shipment is now sent back to GunBroker.

#### Fixed

- **Team mail screening.** A screened message keeps its verdict when two checks run at once, and the website's own order notices are no longer filed under a customer.
- **Bound book checks.** Acquisitions marked [NOT A&D] are no longer flagged by the bound-book checks.
