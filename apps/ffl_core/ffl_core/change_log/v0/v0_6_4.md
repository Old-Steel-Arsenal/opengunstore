### Version 0.6.4 — November 4, 2026

#### New

- **Website customers in the customer list.** Your administrator can now bring the people who bought or opened an account on your website into the POS customer list, so you can look them up at the counter by name, email or phone. Customers you already have, and their past sales, stay as they are.

#### Improved

- **Price changes are noted on the item.** When a manager changes an item's regular price from an add-on such as the sales CRM, the item now shows a note with the old and the new price.
