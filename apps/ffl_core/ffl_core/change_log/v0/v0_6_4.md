### Version 0.6.4 — November 4, 2026

#### New

- **Sales CRM.** Frappe CRM now comes with the POS. Every item shows up in it with the price the register charges, deals are priced from what is in stock, and only a manager can change a price from there. Mail from a customer goes on that customer's deal, and mail from someone new can become a lead.
- **Website customers in the customer list.** Your administrator can now bring the people who bought or opened an account on your website into the POS customer list, so you can look them up at the counter by name, email or phone. Customers you already have, and their past sales, stay as they are.

#### Improved

- **Price changes are noted on the item.** When a manager changes an item's regular price from the CRM, the item now shows a note with the old and the new price.
- **New staff start on the POS.** A new employee who sets their password from the welcome email lands on the store's home page.
- **Quotes stay quotes.** A quote can still be printed and emailed, but it can no longer be turned straight into an order or invoice. Ring the sale at the register or with Create Order, so the paperwork and the books stay right.
