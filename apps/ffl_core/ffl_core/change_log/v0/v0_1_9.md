### Version 0.1.9 — October 21, 2026

#### New

- **Trade in accessories and ammo.** A trade-in can now take accessories and ammo by quantity, on their own or together with a gun, as store credit or paid out.
- **Signing tablet shortcut.** Open the signing tablet at /kiosk. When FastBound's electronic 4473 is on, its idle screen has a Start Form 4473 button.

#### Improved

- **Customers remember their email.** An email typed on a counter order is saved to a customer who has none, so the next order fills it in.
- **Receipt email is optional.** You can create an order without an email; the field reminds you that no receipt is sent without one.
- **Trade-in fills in a known gun.** Picking a gun you have taken in before fills in its make, model and caliber.

#### Fixed

- **A ticket must be paid in full to complete.** If the customer pays part now, save it as a draft and take the rest later from Recent Orders.
- **The browser no longer fills the store login into key and secret fields** on settings and customer forms.
- **A gun that came back and was re-booked by hand in FastBound** now sells without a sync error.
