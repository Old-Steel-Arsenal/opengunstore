### Version 0.3.0 — October 27, 2026

#### New

- **Memberships.** Set up monthly or yearly membership plans and sell them from the Memberships panel on the customer's page. A plan can renew itself on the customer's saved card, with an optional grace period the store chooses when a renewal is declined. Nothing changes until a manager creates a plan.
- **Membership follow-up list.** The Memberships settings page lists members whose renewal is overdue, declined or waiting, with buttons to retry the card or take payment.

#### Improved

- **Counter orders with unsigned agreements.** A counter order can be saved before the customer signs; the agreements are emailed to them, and the gun cannot be handed over until they are signed.
- **Signatures show up at the register right away.** A signature lands on screen as soon as the customer signs, and the register warns you before a page reload would drop the cart.

#### Fixed

- **Restocking from a distributor.** A restock line for a product the store has never carried now creates the product first instead of failing.
