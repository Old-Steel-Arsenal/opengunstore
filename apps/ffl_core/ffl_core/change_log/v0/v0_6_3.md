### Version 0.6.3 — November 3, 2026

#### Fixed

- **Cash Drawer no longer lists the same cash twice.** When an order was paid in cash before a count and completed after it, the Cash Drawer page showed that cash again as a new register sale and took it back off with an equal "Other changes" line. The page now leaves it out, because the drawer already counted it.
- **Register sales post to the books right away more often.** A register sale that ran into another save at the same moment could stay off the books, and send everyone a "could not be consolidated" task, until the automatic retry 15 minutes later. It now posts on the next try.

#### Improved

- **GunBroker seller name is set up once by your administrator.** The seller name moved off the GunBroker Settings page. Your existing name is carried over automatically, so nothing changes in day-to-day listing.
