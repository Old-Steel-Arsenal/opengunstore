### Version 0.10.2 — November 14, 2026

#### Fixed

- **Staff mailboxes in the CRM.** A staff mailbox connected to the CRM could start over from its newest 100 mails on every check and block the other mailboxes while it did, so mail arrived late and the error log filled with mailbox errors. Each mailbox now keeps its place, and one mailbox no longer holds up the others.
