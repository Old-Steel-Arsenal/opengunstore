### Version 0.7.4 — November 9, 2026

#### Fixed

- **Cancelled acquisitions that are still in FastBound.** Cancelling an acquisition used to mark the gun as removed from FastBound even when it was still in the bound book there. The record now says the gun is still in FastBound, and managers get a notice the next day if nobody has dealt with it. If you are amending the record or receiving the gun again, there is nothing to do: the new record links it.
- **Receive Goods when FastBound rejects a gun.** The message now lists the guns from that receipt FastBound had already accepted. Receive again and they are linked automatically.
