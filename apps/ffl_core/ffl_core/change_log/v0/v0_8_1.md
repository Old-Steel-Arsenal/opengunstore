### Version 0.8.1 — November 11, 2026

#### New

- **Skip agreements for one sale.** When a sale is waiting on an unsigned agreement, the Agreements dialog has a Skip for this sale button. A manager approves it with their counter PIN or password, the dialog lists exactly what is being skipped, and the sale gets a note of who approved it. It works once, for the next sale to that customer, within 15 minutes.

#### Fixed

- **FFL transfer guns and the used-gun agreement.** A customer's gun handed over on an FFL transfer is no longer asked for the Used Item Agreement, and the register's Agreements list no longer offers it.

- **Serial number and barcode search.** Counter staff can again scan or type a serial number or barcode into the register's search and get the item with its price, instead of a permission error.
- **Customer email in CRM.** Plain-text emails keep their line breaks instead of running together, links in an email open in a new tab instead of blanking the email, and an email with a long subject no longer shows twice on a lead. Emails already in CRM are tidied up too.
