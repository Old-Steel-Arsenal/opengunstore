### Version 0.7.0 — November 6, 2026

#### New

- **Other mail.** Team mail that is not a customer inquiry is now kept instead of left out, such as supplier mail, automated notices and mail from a blocked domain. Sales Managers see it in the **Other mail** view in CRM, sorted by category. Change a sender's status to **New** to make it an ordinary lead.
- **Spam folder check.** The AI extension can now check a team mailbox's spam folder. A customer's mail comes back into CRM, and real spam leaves no record. A manager turns this on with **Spam folder** in Lead Mail Settings.
- **My follow-ups.** CRM's Leads and Deals pages each have a new view: open records that are yours or have no owner yet, where the customer wrote last, oldest first.
- **Web orders on the deal.** With **Link deals to POS orders** on, a customer's open deal gets a note when their web order is placed, shipped or picked up, cancelled, or refunded.

#### Improved

- **Simpler deal stages.** Deals now use New, Talking, Quoted, Won and Lost. Existing deals move to the matching stage.
- **No quote buttons on deals.** Deals close through **Open order**, so the **Create Quotation** button and the **Quotations** tab are gone.

#### Fixed

- **EZ Check.** A maintenance or error page on the ATF site no longer marks a dealer's license as not found, which could block shipping to them.
