### Version 0.6.7 — November 6, 2026

#### New

- **Open an order from a CRM deal.** A deal can now show who the customer is, what they have bought, and an **Open order** button that fills in Create Order for you. When the order is invoiced, the deal is marked Won with the sale amount. A manager turns this on with **Link deals to POS orders** in Lead Mail Settings.

#### Improved

- **Team mail reaches the CRM faster.** New mail in connected team mailboxes is checked every minute, and a reply sent from the CRM goes out right away.
- **Real names on new contacts.** A contact made from an email now uses the name on the mail instead of the part of the address before the @.

#### Fixed

- **No double entries on the timeline.** The copy of a customer's mail that the POS sends to the person handling it no longer shows up on the timeline a second time.
