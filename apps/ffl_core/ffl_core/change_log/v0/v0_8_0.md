### Version 0.8.0 — November 11, 2026

#### New

- **Want list.** On a CRM deal, *Add want* records what the customer is looking for: a few keywords ("Mosin 91/30"), a specific item, or both. When stock that fits is received or traded in, whoever added the want gets a task. Nothing is emailed to the customer. Close a want from the CRM Want list when it is no longer needed.
- **Website inquiries in CRM.** Price requests, sell-your-gun forms and questions sent from the website arrive in CRM: on the customer's open deal if they have one, otherwise as a lead, with the gun they asked about on it. Needs the website plugin update that sends them.
- **GunBroker buyer questions.** A buyer's question about one of our listings is now its own lead, with the gun attached, instead of sitting with other GunBroker mail. Later messages in the same conversation go to the same lead.

#### Fixed

- **Spam folder check.** Checking the team mailbox's spam folder for customer mail no longer fails to sign in.
