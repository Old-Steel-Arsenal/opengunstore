### Version 0.7.6 — November 10, 2026

#### Fixed

- **Add Stock never uses the selling price as cost.** Adding stock with the cost left blank used to record the item's selling price as what it cost, so every later sale showed a loss. It now uses the cost already on file, and asks you to enter one when there is none. A service should not be stocked at all: untick Maintain Stock on it instead.
- **Web refunds of a whole item.** When a web order refunds a whole item, that item now comes off the sale instead of staying on it at a discount, so the sales report no longer counts its cost against the order. Refunds a person needs to look at, such as an item returned for less than it sold for, wait in the alerts instead of being booked automatically.
