### Version 0.10.1 — November 14, 2026

#### New

- **Sell-your-gun requests in the CRM.** A sell-your-gun request from the website is now its own deal, listed under *Sell requests*. With *Link deals to POS orders* on in Lead Mail Settings, the deal's page shows the gun's details and photos, and you can record your offer and mark it accepted. When the gun arrives, press *Receive gun*: the trade-in dialog opens filled in. Nothing is booked until you submit it. The photos are copied into the POS once the website has the plugin update that allows it; until then the page links to them on the website.
- **Web orders in the CRM.** With *Link deals to POS orders* on, a deal's customer card lists the customer's web orders that are not shipped or picked up yet. A paid web order closes the customer's open lead or deal as won; a cancelled or fully refunded order reopens the deal. Paid web orders already on file do the same once, during the update.
- **Mail sent outside the CRM.** A reply sent from your own mail program now shows on the lead or deal within 10 minutes, and counts as an answer. This covers the mailboxes in Lead Mail Settings.

#### Improved

- **Deals show the person's name.** A deal's page title is the person's name instead of the deal number, and the Deals list shows the name first and has a quick filter for it.
- **Web orders get their customer right away.** A web order is linked to its customer when it comes in, not when it is shipped or picked up. With *Link deals to POS orders* on, a deal with the same email and no customer is linked to that customer by itself.
- **My follow-ups.** A lead or deal leaves the list once it is answered and comes back when the customer writes again.

#### Fixed

- **Point of sale in the sidebar.** Staff now see the *Point of sale* link in the Gun Shop sidebar; before, only the Administrator did.
