### Version 0.6.6 — November 5, 2026

#### New

- **Record Payment on GunBroker orders.** A GunBroker order on Pending Order now has **Record Payment** with the same payment methods as a counter order: credit card, Zelle, ACH, or check and money order. A manager can also key in the buyer's card on the card processor's payment page. Cash is not offered for GunBroker orders. The payment is booked when the order is disposed, as before.
- **Check new team mail before it becomes a lead.** An extension can now look at mail from someone new to the store's shared addresses before the CRM makes a lead. Mail it judges not to be from a customer stays in the mail log with a note saying why. If it guessed wrong, open the mail in the Communication list and click **Make lead**.
