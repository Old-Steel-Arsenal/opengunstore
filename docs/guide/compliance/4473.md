# Form 4473 and NICS

For counter staff running the 4473 on a gun sale, and for managers who handle overrides, redos and delayed checks.

A regulated firearm cannot leave on a sale until a completed Form 4473 and a NICS **Proceed** are on record for every gun on the ticket. [Sell a gun](../counter/sell-a-gun.md) is the counter walk-through.

## Paper or electronic

The store runs one of two ways, set by **Paper 4473 Mode** in [FFL Settings](../admin/ffl-settings.md).

| Route | Set up as | How the form is closed |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic | Paper 4473 Mode off, FastBound on | FastBound tells the system the form is done |
| Paper | Paper 4473 Mode on | The cashier records that the form and check are done |

A change to the mode takes effect on the next page load. Reload any till that is already open, or it keeps the old behavior.

## Statuses

Two fields track a firearm order. They live on the order and on its [disposition](bound-book.md).

- **4473 Status** is `N/A`, `Pending` or `Completed`.
- **NICS Status** is `N/A`, `Proceed`, `Delayed`, `Denied` or `Cancelled`.

The sale completes only when `Completed` and `Proceed` are on record for every gun. Each gun leaves against its own form and check, so a gun added or a serial swapped after the 4473 needs its own. Antiques are exempt and never start a 4473.

The button next to **Complete Order** shows where the order stands:

| Button reads | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **FastBound 4473** | Grey | Nothing started yet. Click to begin. |
| **Awaiting 4473** | Orange | Held. The form is in flight. |
| **4473 Complete** | Green | Cleared. You can complete the order. |
| **Record Paper 4473** | Grey | Paper mode. Click when the form is done. |
| **4473 Recorded** | Green | Paper mode, recorded. |
| **4473 Not Configured** | Red | Neither route is available. See below. |

In electronic mode a refresh control sits beside the button, tooltip "Refresh 4473 status". Use it when FastBound has finished but the screen still says Awaiting. The payment button reads **Paid — waiting for 4473** once money is on a held order, or **Save — waiting for 4473** on a trade-in ticket that takes no money.

## Run the 4473 in FastBound

1. Ring the gun up and take the ticket to payment.
2. Click **FastBound 4473**.
3. Enter the serial for each firearm row.
4. In the FastBound tab that opens, click **Start New 4473** and work through the form with the buyer. The order waits as a draft.
5. Serve other customers meanwhile. Reopen the order later from **Recent Orders → Draft**.
6. When FastBound commits the form, the order turns **4473 Complete**. Click **Complete Order**.

A $0 [pickup ticket](../counter/sell-accessories.md) for a gun ordered to the store runs the same button and the same check.

You can take payment before the check clears; the system warns that the 4473/NICS has not cleared. If the buyer is denied, a manager refunds and deletes the draft.

If the serial on a ticket line does not match the one the 4473 was run on, the system posts a warning on the order. Check the serial against FastBound and correct the ticket before you complete it.

## Record a paper 4473

With **Paper 4473 Mode** on, the button reads **Record Paper 4473**.

1. Complete the paper 4473 with the buyer.
2. Run the background check by phone or the FBI portal.
3. On the payment screen, click **Record Paper 4473**.
4. Read the dialog. For every firearm on the order you confirm that the ATF Form 4473 is completed and signed, that the background check came back Proceed, and that the form is filed with your records.
5. Click **Yes — 4473 completed**.
6. Click **Complete Order**.

Recording it books the disposition for every gun on the ticket under your name and the time, and it cannot be undone from the counter. Any counter role can record it.

!!! warning "Federal recordkeeping"
    Paper mode changes only where the 4473 record lives. It relaxes nothing the ATF requires. The form must still be completed, the check still run and the paperwork still filed exactly as before.

An order already sent to FastBound cannot be closed with a paper form: the refusal is **Electronic 4473 In Flight**. After a [Redo 4473](#redo-4473), the paper route is open again.

## Redo 4473

For a Sales Manager or System Manager, when a completed 4473 has to be done again: the wrong serial was on the form, or the disposition was voided in FastBound.

Clicking the button on a completed order opens **4473 already complete**, where a manager also sees **Redo 4473**.

1. Void the disposition in FastBound first. The system does not touch FastBound.
2. On the held order, click the 4473 button, then **Redo 4473**.
3. Tick **I have already voided this disposition in FastBound**.
4. Type a **Reason (recorded on the order)**.
5. Click **Redo 4473**.

Without the tick the system stops with `Void the disposition in FastBound first, then redo the 4473.` Without a reason it stops with `A reason is required to redo a completed 4473.`

The order's retail-sale dispositions are cancelled, the order goes back to 4473 `Pending` with no NICS result, and the reason is written on it. Its links to FastBound and the signed PDF are cleared, so refresh cannot bring the old status back. A paper or override [4473 record](4473-records.md) for it is marked NICS `Cancelled`.

Then run the corrected form in FastBound, record it on paper, or use the manager override. Any of the three records the sale again, even on an order first sent to FastBound.

## Manager override

For a Sales Manager or System Manager, when the 4473 and NICS were genuinely completed in FastBound but never synced back: a serial edited in a shared account, a deleted disposition, FastBound down. You work from the order, not the register, and need a written reason.

1. In the sidebar, open **Sales → POS invoices** and open the held order.
2. Click **Manager Override 4473**. It shows on any draft sale with a firearm whose 4473 is not `Completed`, including one that reads `N/A` because the form was run straight in FastBound.
3. Read the dialog, **Manager Override — Force-Complete 4473**. It is only for a 4473 and NICS genuinely completed in FastBound. It books an audited override record with your name and reason, and it does **not** update FastBound.
4. Type the **Reason**.
5. Click **Force-complete the sale**.

The system books one retail-sale disposition per gun, marked as an override with your name and reason, and writes a [4473 record](4473-records.md) with Source `Paper / override`. A double click still books one per gun. Reconcile FastBound separately.

!!! danger "An override is not a lesser record"
    An override satisfies the sale gate exactly like a FastBound-committed disposition, and nothing downstream treats it differently. It is a control only because a manager reads it back. Every override and every paper 4473 lands on the **Hand-Asserted 4473 Dispositions** report: see [Daily checks](daily-checks.md#3-hand-asserted-4473-dispositions).

## When neither route is available

With FastBound switched off and Paper 4473 Mode also off, the button turns red and reads **4473 Not Configured**. Clicking it opens **No 4473 route configured**: the counter has no way to record a 4473 for the sale.

Nothing at the counter fixes this. A manager either turns on **Paper 4473 Mode** in [FFL Settings](../admin/ffl-settings.md) or ticks **Enable FastBound** again in [FastBound](../admin/fastbound.md). For a single order, a manager can still use the override, which works in this setup.

## Delayed checks and the Brady date

A `Delayed` NICS result means the sale is on hold, not refused. Federal law lets a dealer transfer at their discretion once the third business day has passed without a denial.

The **Pending 4473 Orders** report prints that date as **Brady Day 3**. It steps over Saturdays and Sundays but not holidays, so check the calendar before you rely on it. Working the report, and clearing an abandoned order, is on [Daily checks](daily-checks.md#1-pending-4473-orders).

## When a result comes back

Every NICS result on a sale rings the [bell](../start/dashboard.md) of each Sales User, Sales Manager and System Manager, for example "NICS Delayed — <buyer> (<order>)" or "Delayed NICS cleared: Proceed — …". Nothing is emailed to staff.

When a delayed check proceeds, the buyer is emailed once with the **NICS Cleared Pickup** template, at the order's contact address or the customer's email. Nothing is sent without an address, so call the customer if the gun matters.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting 4473** on an order you believe is done | Click the refresh control beside the button. |
| `FastBound has not sent the completed 4473 back yet.` | Check the form is committed in FastBound, then refresh again. |
| `Could not reach FastBound — checked the local records only. Still waiting on the 4473.` | Try again. If it persists, a manager checks [FastBound](../admin/fastbound.md). |
| `Cannot submit firearm transfer: 4473 form status is '<status>'…` or `NICS status is '<status>'…` | The form or the check is not done. The message ends with the next step for your store's route. A Delayed order waits; a Denied one does not complete. |
| `Cannot submit: firearm serial(s) <serials> have no completed 4473 with NICS Proceed on this order.` | A gun was added or swapped after the 4473. Run a 4473 for that gun. |
