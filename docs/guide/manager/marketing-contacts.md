# Marketing contacts

For store managers, when you need to see who gets the store's marketing email, stop it for someone, or decide whether store customers are subscribed.

A marketing contact is one email address with a status that says whether the store may send it marketing email. Contacts come from the website's sign-up forms, from back-in-stock requests, and, if you turn it on, from your own customers.

## Before you start

- **Marketing contacts** is in the sidebar under **Customers** once an administrator turns marketing on in **Marketing Settings**. Until then it is hidden.
- Everyone who sells can open the list. Only managers see the **Unsubscribe** and **Resubscribe** buttons.

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | Gets marketing email |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscribed** | The person signed up, or is a store customer and the store subscribes them | Yes |
| **Not Subscribed** | The address is on file, for example from a back-in-stock request, but the person never signed up | No |
| **Unsubscribed** | The person, or a manager, stopped the email | No |
| **Bounced** | The address does not work | No |
| **Complained** | The person marked a message as spam | No |

A contact only moves toward **Unsubscribed**, **Bounced** or **Complained**. A website form, an import or a customer record never moves it back. Only a manager can, with a reason.

## Stop email for someone

1. In the sidebar, open **Customers**, then **Marketing contacts**.
2. Open the person's contact.
3. Click **Unsubscribe**.
4. Type a reason if you want to keep one. Click **Unsubscribe** in the box, or close it to cancel.

The status changes right away and the contact's **Activity** list records who did it and why. The status field cannot be edited by hand.

## Put someone back

A manager can put a contact that is **Unsubscribed**, **Bounced** or **Complained** back to **Subscribed**.

1. Open the contact and click **Resubscribe**.
2. Type why the person may be emailed again. The reason is required.
3. Click **Resubscribe** in the box.

Do this only when the person asked to be emailed again. A bounced address can bounce again.

## What a contact shows

- **Waiting requests** lists the back-in-stock and model reminders the person still has open.
- **Activity** lists what happened to the contact, newest first: sign-ups, status changes, messages sent. It shows the latest 20. **All activity** opens the full list.
- The list opens on **Subscribed** contacts. Filter by **Status**, **Source** or **Customer** above the list, and by tag on the left.
- On a CRM deal, the customer card has a **Marketing email:** line with the customer's status and tags.

## Subscribe store customers

In **Marketing Settings**, **Subscribe store customers** is off. Ask your administrator to open **Settings**, then **Marketing Settings** under **POS & Sales**, if you want it on.

When it is on, saving a customer who has an email address puts that address on the list as **Subscribed**, tagged **in-store** and **new-arrivals**. Customers are not asked first.

- An address that is already a contact keeps its status. A person who unsubscribed stays unsubscribed.
- The store's own email domain is left out.
- When a customer's email changes, an unsubscribe on the old address carries over to the new one. A subscription does not.
- Customers who were already on file are not added by turning it on. Ask your administrator to list them in one pass.

Turn it on only if your store treats a sale as agreement to receive its email.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Marketing contacts** is not in the sidebar | Ask your administrator to turn on **Enabled** in **Marketing Settings** |
| No **Unsubscribe** button | Only managers see it. A contact that is already **Unsubscribed**, **Bounced** or **Complained** shows **Resubscribe** instead |
| A new email address for a customer shows **Unsubscribed** | The old address had unsubscribed. Resubscribe only if the customer asks |
| A customer has no marketing line on the deal | The customer's address is not a contact yet, or two customers share it |
