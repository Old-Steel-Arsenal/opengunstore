# Answer a customer with a drafted reply

For sales staff, when a customer's mail or website question waits for an answer on a CRM lead or deal.

**Draft reply** writes a reply to the customer's message with the register's prices and what is in stock now. You read it, change what you need, and send it yourself. Nothing reaches the customer until you send it.

## Before you start

- **Draft reply** shows only when the store's AI extension drafts replies. Ask your administrator to set it up.
- You need to be able to change the lead or deal. On a record you can only read, there is no draft for you.

## Steps

1. Open the lead or deal.
2. To answer one mail, open the reply on that mail first, so the mail is quoted. Then click **Draft reply** at the top of the page. The draft goes in above the quoted mail.
3. With no reply open, click **Draft reply**. The **Reply to** box opens with the customer's address, the subject and the draft, with your signature under it.
4. Read the draft. Check each price and each gun it names.
5. Send it from the reply, or click **Send** in the box.

A draft can end with **Needs a person:** and what the customer asked that a person must answer. Answer that part yourself.

## Drafts ready before you open the record

With **Draft replies as mail arrives** on in [Lead Mail Settings](../manager/settings.md#lead-mail-settings), the POS writes the reply a few minutes after the customer's message comes in. The button then reads **Draft ready**, and clicking it fills in that draft at once.

- The draft answers the customer's newest message. When the customer writes again, a new one is written. When the store sends a mail on the record, it goes.
- When a price or the stock of a product in the draft has changed since, the button reads **Draft reply** again, and clicking it writes a new draft.
- A draft written from mail or notes you cannot open is not shown to you. Clicking writes one from what you can see.
- Other mail, closed leads and deals, sell requests and mail from a machine get no draft ahead.

## What happens next

- Your reply shows on the record's timeline like any mail the store sends.
- Sending a reply removes the draft that was waiting.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Fix |
|---|---|
| No **Draft reply** button | Ask your administrator to set up the AI extension |
| **Couldn't write a draft. Reply as usual.** | Write the reply yourself |
| **No mail from the customer to reply to.** | The record has nothing from the customer yet |
| A limit or other reason from the AI | Write the reply yourself, or try later |
