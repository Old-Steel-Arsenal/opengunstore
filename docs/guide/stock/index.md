# Stock

For inventory staff and managers, when guns and goods come in, get priced, get put away, get counted or need fixing.

Counter staff take trade-ins and use the store map. Stock managers receive goods, finish counts and use the correction tools.

In the sidebar, the **Inventory** group holds **Items**, **Serial numbers**, **Stock on hand**, **Inventory count** and **Storage locations**. **Receive goods** and **Distributor orders** sit under **Purchasing**. The [Dashboard](../start/dashboard.md) also has a **Receive goods** button.

## Guns and everything else

Every firearm is tracked by its serial number, without exception. The bound book records physical guns, so a gun cannot be received without a serial, and the bulk stock tools never touch one.

Ammunition, holsters, cleaning kits and gun parts are counted by quantity, with no serial numbers. [Models, serials and prices](items-and-serials.md) explains how the two are kept.
