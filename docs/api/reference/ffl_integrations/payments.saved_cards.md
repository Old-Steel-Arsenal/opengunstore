## `charge_saved_card`

`POST /api/method/ffl_integrations.payments.saved_cards.charge_saved_card`

**Auth (derived from code):** Roles: Sales Manager / System Manager

Charge a customer's saved card against a submitted Sales Invoice (merchant-initiated).

Refused before the gateway when the card was removed or is another customer's, the invoice
is not submitted or owes nothing, or the amount exceeds the balance. The approval books a
Payment Entry exactly like a terminal payment; a decline books nothing. An answer that never
came back locks the invoice against another saved-card charge until a later call looks the
charge up at the gateway and records it.

| arg | type | required | default | description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sales_invoice` | string | yes | — | Submitted Sales Invoice with an outstanding balance. |
| `saved_card` | string | yes | — | Saved Card name (must be Active and the invoice customer's). |
| `amount` | — | no | `None` | Amount to charge, at most the outstanding balance (blank = the whole balance). |

**Returns:** {ok, status, unique_reference, approval_code, amount, payment_entry, outstanding, paid} on approval; {ok: False, status, error} on a decline; resolved=True when an earlier charge was settled instead of a new one.

Remove a saved card: the card processor forgets it, then the record turns Removed.
